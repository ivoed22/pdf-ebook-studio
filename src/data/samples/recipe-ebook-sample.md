title: Lemon Herb Chicken
subtitle: A bright and simple chicken dish with lemon, garlic and herbs.

prepTime: 10 min
cookTime: 25 min
totalTime: 35 min
servings: 2
difficulty: Easy

### Ingredients
- 2 chicken breasts
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- salt and pepper

### Steps
1. Preheat the oven.
2. Mix lemon juice, olive oil, garlic and herbs.
3. Coat the chicken.
4. Bake until cooked through.
5. Rest and serve.

### Notes
Serve with roasted potatoes, salad or warm pita.
