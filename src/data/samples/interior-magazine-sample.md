### Why It Works
The deep walnut wall, integrated shelving, tailored upholstery and cognac lounge chair give the space a strong masculine-luxe identity while still feeling calm and elegant.

### How To Recreate
Start with one dominant material story: rich walnut or dark stained wood. Pair it with taupe upholstery, warm integrated lighting, a leather chair and minimal decorative objects.
